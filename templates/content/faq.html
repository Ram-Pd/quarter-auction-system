Do I need an account to see the quarters?

No. The quarter list, bidding slots and results are public. You need an account to register for bidding and to bid.

Why do I have to confirm my email?

All documents and results are sent by email. We send a 6-digit code, valid for {{ OTP_VALID_MINUTES }} minutes, to make sure the address is yours.

Can I apply for more quarters later?

Yes, until {{ APPLICATION_CLOSES_HOURS_BEFORE_AUCTION }} hours before that quarter's bidding slot starts. You get a supplementary Intimation Slip for the added quarters only.

How much is the EMD?

Each quarter has its own EMD amount, shown in the quarter list and on your Intimation Slip. Pay it separately for each quarter.

When can I start bidding?

When the auction office has checked your documents (your status becomes Active) and verified the EMD receipt for that quarter. Your status is shown after you log in.

What is a bid step?

The minimum raise: {{ DEFAULT_INCREMENT_PERCENT|floatformat:"-2" }}% of the base rate. For a base rate of Rs. 45,000 the step is Rs. 90. Quick buttons bid +1, +2 or +5 steps; you can also type any higher amount.

What happens if my internet drops during bidding?

The page reconnects automatically and refreshes the highest bid and the time left. A bid is only placed when the page says it was accepted.

Will other bidders know who I am?

No. Everyone sees only codes such as "Bidder 3". Your Aadhaar and PAN are stored encrypted and shown only masked.

What if nobody bids on a quarter?

The quarter stays vacant and may be auctioned again later.
