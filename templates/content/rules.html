Placeholder rules for this training project, not official SAIL policy.

  1. Create an account with your email (confirmed by a one-time code), then log in.
  2. Click Register for bidding: give your details, Aadhaar and PAN, and select the quarters you want. You may apply for as many quarters as you like, but you can be allotted at most {{ MAX_QUARTERS_PER_APPLICANT }}.
  3. You receive an Application Form and an Intimation Slip by email. The slip shows your URN and the EMD (earnest money deposit) for each quarter you selected.
  4. Pay the EMD for each quarter through SBI Collect using your URN, then bring your original documents and the receipts to the auction office.
  5. You can bid on a quarter only when your registration is active and that quarter's EMD is verified. Paying the EMD for one quarter gives no right to bid on another.
  6. Each quarter has its own bidding slot (usually {{ DEFAULT_AUCTION_DURATION_MINUTES }} minutes). Several slots may run at the same time.
  7. The first bid must be at least the base rate. Every later bid must beat the current highest bid by at least one step ({{ DEFAULT_INCREMENT_PERCENT|floatformat:"-2" }}% of the base rate, rounded to whole rupees). Bids are in whole rupees.
  8. Bidders appear only as anonymous codes such as "Bidder 7". Your name is never shown to anyone else.
  9. When the slot ends, the highest valid bid wins. If the highest bidder has already been allotted {{ MAX_QUARTERS_PER_APPLICANT }} quarters, the next-highest eligible bidder wins instead.
  10. The winner is informed by email. The result is published on the auction page.
